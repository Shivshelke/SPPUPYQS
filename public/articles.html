How to Prepare for SPPU Engineering Exams: The 2024 Pattern

Published on September 15, 2026 • By SYNAPSE Team

Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) engineering exams are notorious for their vast syllabus and challenging questions. With the introduction of the 2024 pattern, students must adapt their preparation strategies to score well.

1. Understand the Syllabus Thoroughly

Before diving into books, download the official 2024 pattern syllabus for your respective branch (Computer, IT, Mechanical, Civil, etc.). The new pattern emphasizes outcome-based education (OBE) and practical applications. Pay attention to the weightage of each unit.

2. Analyze Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

This is where SYNAPSE comes in. PYQs are the most reliable way to understand the examiner's mindset. While the 2024 pattern is relatively new, topics from the 2019 and 2015 patterns often overlap. Focus on the core concepts that are tested repeatedly. Download the In-Sem and End-Sem papers available on our portal and solve them under timed conditions.

3. Prioritize Your Study Modules

Don't try to study everything at once. Divide your subjects into strong and weak areas. Tackle the high-weightage units first. For numerical subjects like Engineering Mathematics (M1, M2, M3) or Mechanics, daily practice is essential. For theoretical subjects, creating concise notes or mind maps works best.

4. Use Standard Reference Books

While local publications might seem easy for last-minute prep, standard reference books provide a deeper understanding, which is crucial for the conceptual questions often asked in the 2024 pattern. Supplement your reading with video lectures for complex topics.

Consistency is key. Start your preparation early, utilize the resources on SYNAPSE, and you'll be well on your way to acing your SPPU exams!

The Importance of In-Sem Exams and How to Ace Them

Published on August 28, 2026 • By SYNAPSE Team

Many engineering students make the mistake of not taking their In-Semester (In-Sem) exams seriously. However, in the SPPU grading system, these 30 marks can significantly impact your final CGPA. Here is why they matter and how you can maximize your score.

Why In-Sem Exams Matter

  • Easier Scoring Opportunity: The syllabus for In-Sem exams usually covers only the first two or three units. This is a much smaller volume of information to retain compared to the End-Sem exams.
  • Buffer for End-Sem: A high score in the In-Sem takes the pressure off the final exam. If you secure 20+ out of 30, you only need a reasonable score in the End-Sem to pass comfortably and secure a good grade.
  • Early Concept Validation: In-Sem exams test your foundational understanding. Performing well ensures you are on the right track before moving to more advanced topics.

Strategies for In-Sem Success

Start studying at least two weeks before the exam. Focus heavily on class notes and assignments given by your professors, as they often frame the In-Sem questions. Review the PYQs specific to the first few units. Since the time is limited (usually 1 hour), practice writing concise and precise answers. Don't write unnecessary details; stick to the point and use diagrams wherever applicable.

Remember, every mark counts towards your final degree. Treat your In-Sem exams with the seriousness they deserve.
