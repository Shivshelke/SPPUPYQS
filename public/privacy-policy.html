Welcome to SYNAPSE SPPU PYQ Portal ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit sppupyq.vercel.app.

SYNAPSE is intended for college students (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
