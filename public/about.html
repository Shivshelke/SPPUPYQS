🎯 Our Mission

Every SPPU student deserves easy access to previous year question papers — without hunting through Telegram groups, unreliable drives, or paid sites. SYNAPSE was built to solve exactly that. We organize every PYQ by year, branch, semester, and subject so you can find what you need in seconds.

Our Story

SYNAPSE was created by SPPU engineering students who were frustrated with scattered PYQ resources across the internet. We built this platform to give every student — regardless of their college or resources — a fair shot at exam preparation.

We are constantly updating the portal with new papers as they become available from SPPU. If you can't find a paper, use our feedback form and we'll add it as soon as possible.

Have suggestions or want to contribute? Reach us via the Contact page.
